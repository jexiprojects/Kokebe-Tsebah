Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about admissions, academics, and school life at Kokebe Tsibah.

Kokebe Tsibah provides comprehensive education for Grades 9 through 12. In preparatory grades (11 and 12), students follow Ministry of Education accredited curricula in either the Natural Science or Social Science stream.

To ensure manageable classroom sizes and dedicated teacher mentorship, the school operates a morning shift and an afternoon shift. Shift schedules are organized by grade levels at the start of each academic calendar year.

Our campus features dedicated natural science laboratories for chemistry, physics, and biology, an ICT computer center, and a quiet reference library with extensive matriculation exam preparation archives.

Alumni and current students may visit our administrative office along Comoros Street in Yeka Sub-City during working hours (Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM), or email us directly at info@kokebetsibah.edu.et.

The school is located on Comoros Street, Yeka Sub-City, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, easily accessible by public transportation and local transit.
